# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Client management with per-client currency and tax rates
- Time tracking with hours, rate, date, and billed/unbilled status
- Invoice creation from line items or tracked time entries
- Auto-numbered invoices (INV-1001, INV-1002, ...)
- Professional printable HTML invoices with print-to-PDF
- Invoice status tracking: draft → sent → paid / overdue
- Business info stored per project
- Overdue invoice detection
- CSV export for invoices and time entries
- Dashboard with paid, outstanding, and unbilled hour stats
- Zero dependencies — Node.js built-ins only
